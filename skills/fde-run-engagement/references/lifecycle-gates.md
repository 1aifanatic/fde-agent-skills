# FDE Lifecycle Gates

Stages advance through evidence and named accountability, not agent confidence.

## 1. Framing

Required:

- problem/outcome and measurable baseline plan;
- scope and non-goals;
- sponsor, process owner, delivery owner;
- data/access boundary and known constraints;
- engagement success and stop conditions.

Approval: sponsor or delegated process owner.

## 2. Discovery

Required:

- evidence inventory and stakeholder coverage;
- process boundary, trigger, end condition;
- normal path plus material exception inventory;
- system/data/control map;
- high-impact knowledge gaps assigned.

Advance when every critical unknown is evidenced, contradicted with resolver, or owned with an approved deferral.

## 3. Current-state review

Required:

- step-level map with ownership, inputs, outputs, rules, systems, timing, controls;
- documented versus observed differences;
- exception/escalation/rework loops;
- baseline metrics and evidence confidence;
- no unresolved contradiction that could materially change design.

Approval: customer process owner and delivery owner.

## 4. Future-state design

Required:

- disposition for every current step;
- retained human accountability;
- controls, exception handling, fallback, source-of-truth behavior;
- value hypothesis and adoption impact;
- risk/security assessment plan.

Approval: process owner; security/control owners for affected obligations.

## 5. Solution design

Required:

- requirements and acceptance criteria;
- architecture and project/component boundaries;
- data, integration, security, privacy, observability, operational decisions;
- traceability initialized;
- implementable backlog and environment plan.

Approval: architecture/delivery owner and required specialists.

## 6. Build and verification

Required:

- implementation evidence linked to requirements;
- red-capable tests and evaluation fixtures;
- deterministic, integration, edge, security, recovery, and regression results;
- known defects and accepted residual risks;
- versioned artifacts and configuration.

Advance when no critical requirement is orphaned or unverified.

## 7. UAT and release review

Required:

- named business acceptance;
- release package/version and deployment evidence;
- access/configuration readiness;
- monitoring, runbook, support, rollback/compensation;
- security/governance approval and outcome measurement plan.

Approval: business, release, and required security/control owners.

## 8. Operating

Required:

- production health and outcome evidence;
- incident and support ownership;
- retained audit evidence;
- review dates for stale knowledge;
- governed change intake.

## 9. Change review

Required:

- attributable and authorized request;
- approved-baseline comparison and complete impact analysis;
- exact patch, tests, rollout, monitoring, rollback;
- reapproval when the patch changes;
- post-release documentation and traceability alignment.

## Mandatory stop conditions

Stop advancement for missing approval, unresolved high-impact contradiction, unsupported material claim, unclear authority, security/compliance uncertainty, irreversible high-severity action, failed traceability/safety evaluation, ambiguous external state, or suspected cross-engagement contamination.
