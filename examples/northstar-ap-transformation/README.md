# Worked Example: Northstar AP Transformation

This is a fully synthetic engagement used to demonstrate all six FDE skills. Northstar Manufacturing, its people, systems, email addresses, volumes, measurements, and outcomes are fictional. Nothing here represents observed production results.

## Scenario

Northstar's accounts-payable team handles 1,200 invoices per month. Eighteen percent enter an exception path, creating an average 6.2-business-day exception cycle, 28 hours of weekly manual triage, and repeated routing disputes. The engagement asks whether AI and automation can shorten the cycle without weakening payment controls or replacing the existing ERP.

## What the demonstration proves

- The engagement can be resumed from files without chat history.
- Interview questions are driven by decision gaps rather than a generic questionnaire.
- Claims remain linked to evidence and contradictions remain visible until resolved.
- Current-state exceptions and controls shape the future-state design.
- Delivery requirements trace through planned components and tests.
- A casual email change becomes an authorized, testable, reversible change instead of an immediate production edit.

## What it does not prove

The example does not prove real-world ROI, model accuracy, ERP compatibility, or production readiness. The target metrics are design hypotheses that would require implementation, evaluation, UAT, release approval, and production measurement.
