# Synthetic Source S-001: AP Invoice Exception SOP

- **Owner:** Northstar AP Operations
- **Effective date:** 2026-01-15
- **Classification:** Synthetic demonstration data

## Documented procedure

1. AP specialists monitor ap@northstar.example every two hours.
2. Each invoice is registered in LedgerOne and checked against a purchase order and receipt.
3. Missing or inconsistent records are recorded in the AP Exception Tracker.
4. The specialist emails Procurement Operations or the receiving department and waits for correction.
5. Every invoice above USD 10,000 is sent to the Controller for approval.
6. When all checks pass, the specialist marks the invoice ready for payment.

## Documented controls

- The person correcting vendor or purchase-order data cannot approve payment.
- Suspected duplicates must be held for manual review.
- Exception evidence must be retained for seven years.

## Known limitation

This document describes the intended path. It does not describe retry behavior, ownership when email is unanswered, or the actual exception categories used by specialists.
